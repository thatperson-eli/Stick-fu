MOMENTUM

Keep running to build speed. Faster hits land harder.
Jump into a wall and hold toward it to run up it. Tap jump to wall-jump.
Throws beat blocks and use the target's own speed. Smash bodies into pillars and the bridge to break them.

SPECIAL WHEEL

Hold Space (or the SPECIAL button on phones) and time slows. Let go to do the move.
Inner ring (drag a little, or tap a direction once): uppercut, flip kick, bicycle, dive, sweep, roundhouse, chi blast, throw.
Outer ring (drag further, or tap the same direction twice): chi supers you can only do from the wheel. Dragon Palm, Tornado Kick, Hundred Hands, Shadow Step, Earth Stomp and Iron Body cost half a chi bar; Chi Burst and Chi Grip need a full one.
Tap without choosing for a quick special that suits the moment.

SPECIAL MOVES (COMBOS)

Uppercut: block + punch. Launches them into the air.
Sweep: block + kick. Low, goes under blocks, trips them.
Dive kick: in the air, down + kick. Harder the faster you fall.
Chi burst: block + chi with a full bar. Blasts everything away.
Roundhouse: spin key. Slow to start, hits hardest.
Flip kick: roundhouse key in the air. Heel drop that goes through a block.
Bicycle kick: in the air, hold jump + kick. Three kicks, the last launches.
Chi grip: block + throw with a completely full chi bar. Lift them, then slam them down.
Balance: hits knock you off balance. Take too many and you stumble, and a stumbling fighter goes flying from the next good hit. Blocking strong hits wears your guard down until it breaks.

WEAPONS

Arenas 2–5 drop a weapon mid-round. Run over it to grab it.
Staff: punch thrusts further, roundhouse sweeps both sides.
Bow: hold punch to draw, let go to fire. Running speed adds to arrow speed.
Get sent flying and you drop it.

ROAD WAR (CHAPTER 7)

Speed D or → · Brake A or ← · Jump W or ↑
Fire: hold J or Z. The roof cannon aims itself.
Bomb: K or X. Lob it onto barricades, mines and bikes.
Nitro: L or C with half a chi bar. Ram straight through bikes and barricades.
Red rings on the road show where bombs will land. Jump or change speed.

THE RIDGE (CHAPTER 8)

Aim with the mouse or the move keys (drag on phones) · Fire click, J or Z
Scope right-click (hold), or K / X to switch · Hold breath Space, Shift, L or C
Far targets drift with the wind. Watch the flag and aim into it.

PAUSE & SOUND

P or Esc pauses (the pause button on screen on phones). Restart, quit, or switch music and sound effects off from there. The game pauses itself if you switch apps.

STORY MODE

Twelve chapters: ten fights against named rivals, a vehicle chase and a sniper stage. You start with punch, kick and block, and every chapter you win teaches you a new move. VS CPU and 2-player use every arena you've reached in the story, with all moves.
